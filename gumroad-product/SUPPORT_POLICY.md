## Refund Policy

### 30-day satisfaction guarantee — Starter tier

If you purchased the Starter tier and the onboarding guide does not help you achieve a
running local environment within 30 days, email `[YOUR_EMAIL]` with a description of
where you got stuck. If the issue is in the guide itself (a step is wrong, missing, or
misleading), you qualify for a full refund, no questions asked.

### Pro and Team tiers

Refunds for Pro and Team tiers are handled case by case. If you have not yet used your
email support credit or screen-share session and request a refund within 14 days of
purchase, a full refund will be issued. After the support credit has been used, or after
14 days, refunds are at the seller's discretion.

### What refunds do NOT cover

- Changing your mind after downloading the source code
- Deciding the tech stack is not right for your use case (all stack information is
  disclosed in the listing before purchase)
- Inability to run the software due to your own infrastructure constraints or third-party
  service accounts you have not created

### How to request a refund

Email `[YOUR_EMAIL]` with:
1. Your Gumroad order number
2. The tier you purchased
3. A brief description of the issue

Refunds are processed via Gumroad within 5 business days of approval.
